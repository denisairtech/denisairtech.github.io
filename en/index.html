About

ד.ד. מיזוג אוויר (D.D. Mizug Avir) is an Israeli air-conditioning business owned by Denis Zolotar, an air conditioner repair technician. Under the Denis Air Tech brand we develop tools and apps for AC technicians.

Hands-on field experience is the basis of our tools: capacity calculations, unit conversion and client documents — simple, fast and clear.
