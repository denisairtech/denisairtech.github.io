About

ד.ד. מיזוג אוויר (D.D. Mizug Avir) is an Israeli air-conditioning business owned by Denis Zolotar, an air conditioner repair technician. Under the Denis Air Tech brand we develop tools and apps for AC technicians.

Hands-on field experience is the basis of our tools: capacity calculations, unit conversion and client documents — simple, fast and clear.

Apps

ClimaSnap IL

AC capacity calculator for Israel

A professional AC capacity calculator for a room or a whole apartment / house, tuned to Israel’s climate. With tools for the technician and client jobs — all on the phone, even offline.

  • Room or apartment / house: named rooms (living room, Mamad…) and a whole-home total
  • BTU · tons · kW · כ״ס (HP) · CFM
  • Region (incl. Eilat / Arava) and sun side, incl. diagonals
  • Send PDF · summary and image via WhatsApp
  • Saved jobs with notes and photos · backup and restore
  • Unit converter · cable cross-section ↔ current (2014 Electricity Regulations)
  • Duct sizing: air speed and mixed diameters
  • Hebrew · Russian · English · dark theme
  • Works offline · no ads, no tracking

AirMaster

In development · coming soon

Client, job schedule & finance manager for AC technicians

A field-service job book and client manager built for air-conditioning technicians and installers: daily, weekly and monthly schedule, client cards with addresses and AC units, price catalog and PDF quotes, income, expense and net profit tracking — all on the phone, 100% offline.

  • Job calendar (Day / Week / Month): hourly timeline, job statuses and working hours
  • Client cards & addresses: import from phone contacts, one-tap call, WhatsApp and Waze / Google Maps navigation
  • Customer AC units: brand, model, BTU capacity, refrigerant type, serial number and warranty tracking
  • Service-due reminders: automatic list of AC units due for cleaning / maintenance with WhatsApp templates
  • Services & materials catalog: built-in price list with technician cost, plus “before” and “after” site photos
  • PDF quotes & receipts: send a formatted text summary or PDF document via WhatsApp in one tap
  • Finance & net profit: income, categorized expenses with receipt photos, partial payments and client debts
  • Statistics & period reports: revenue charts and export to PDF, Excel (XLSX) and CSV
  • 4 languages & 5 currencies: Hebrew (RTL) · Russian · Ukrainian · English · ₪ · $ · € · ₽ · ₴
  • 100% offline · no ads, accounts or cloud · full database and photo backup to a single file
